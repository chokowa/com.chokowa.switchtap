1. Provider

SwitchTap (the “App”) is provided by Chokowa Studio.

2. Information used by the App

The App reads the names, package names and icons of installed apps that can be launched. It uses this information to let you select and display the apps you want to switch between.

The App stores your set names, colors, app order, display layout, initial setup progress, button positions, language preferences and notification settings on your device.

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply and handle your inquiry.

3. Information sent outside your device

The App has no feature that sends your sets or installed-app list to a Chokowa Studio server. It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

The App uses Google Play Billing when you buy or restore additional sets and when it checks purchase status at launch or on return to the App. Google Play processes billing information such as product details, purchase status and purchase tokens. The App uses purchase status to enable purchased features. Chokowa Studio does not directly receive your payment details.

4. Permissions

Permission to display over other apps is used to show app-switching buttons. Notification permission is used to show the status of the floating buttons and related controls.

5. Storage and deletion

Sets and settings are stored on your device. You can delete sets within the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data. An active Google Play purchase can be checked after reinstallation with the Google account used to buy it.

We keep support emails as needed to handle inquiries and delete them when no longer needed. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

6. Third-party services

Google Play handles billing. For information about how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to features, services or legal requirements. Important changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
